Privacy Policy for InsightOrbit

InsightOrbit ("we", "our", or "us") is an automated daily business intelligence and news research tool. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to user privacy and explains how information is handled.

1. Information We Collect

Our application integrates with Google OAuth strictly to interact with your Gmail account for composing draft emails. We do not collect, harvest, store, or transmit your emails, credentials, or personal identification data to any external server.

2. Use of Google User Data

The requested scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.compose) is used solely to compose daily commercial stories and news reports as drafts within your Gmail mailbox. We never read, modify, or delete your existing inbox emails, nor do we send emails automatically without your review and explicit submission.

3. Data Storage & Security

OAuth tokens are kept strictly private within your secure environment (such as GitHub Secrets for automated runs) and are loaded only during scheduled execution. We do not use third-party tracking scripts, advertising SDKs, or data analytics.

4. Contact & Developer Information

For questions or inquiries regarding this application and privacy practices, please contact the repository maintainer on GitHub.
